DECODE (2025–2026 FIRST Tech Challenge, presented by RTX) — condensed official game-manual facts (Section 10, manual V9) for judging a robot's intake/launcher. A MATCH is 30s autonomous (AUTO) + a 2:00 driver-controlled period (TELEOP).

ARTIFACTS (the game pieces): 5-inch diameter balls (spec ~4.9 in ±0.25 in at the mold seam), ~0.165 lb (75 g), hard perforated plastic. Two colors, PURPLE and GREEN. 36 are staged on the field (24 purple, 12 green). A robot may preload and carry up to 3 at a time.

Scoring: robots COLLECT artifacts and score them into their alliance GOAL — an elevated structure with an open top, so robots typically LAUNCH artifacts in; the intake feeds the launcher. To count, an artifact must enter the GOAL through the open top, exit under the archway, and pass the diverting SQUARE:
- CLASSIFIED (3 pts): passes the SQUARE and drops directly onto the RAMP.
- OVERFLOW (1 pt): passes the SQUARE but misses the classified path.
- DEPOT (1 pt, TELEOP): an artifact left over the alliance's DEPOT.

MOTIF / PATTERN: before the match an OBELISK displays an AprilTag that selects the MOTIF — GPP (tag 21), PGP (tag 22), or PPG (tag 23). The 3-color motif repeats 3× to set target colors for the 9 RAMP indices. An artifact resting on the RAMP scores PATTERN points (2 pts) when its color, in sequence, matches the motif color for that index (and the GATE retains it). So sorting/ordering purple vs. green and feeding them in the right sequence directly earns points.

LEAVE (3 pts, AUTO): robot fully off its LAUNCH LINE at the end of AUTO.
BASE (endgame): partially returned to BASE = 5 pts, fully returned = 10 pts, plus a 10-pt bonus if both alliance robots fully return.

Point values — AUTO / TELEOP:
  LEAVE 3 / —   ·   CLASSIFIED 3 / 3   ·   OVERFLOW 1 / 1   ·   DEPOT — / 1
  PATTERN (matches motif) 2 / 2   ·   BASE partial — / 5   ·   BASE full — / 10   ·   both-robots bonus — / 10
Ranking points come from Movement (LEAVE+BASE), Goal (artifacts scored through the SQUARE), and Pattern thresholds, plus Win (3) / Tie (1).

Design implications to weigh: rollers, gap, and compliance must suit a light, 5-inch, hard, perforated ball and capture BOTH colors reliably; the mechanism should singulate and feed a launcher at a controlled, repeatable rate (it's a launch-into-an-elevated-goal game); CLASSIFIED (3) is worth 3× OVERFLOW (1), so accurate, controlled scoring matters; and color sorting/ordering to match the motif earns extra PATTERN points.
